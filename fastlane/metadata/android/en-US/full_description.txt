A FOSS Play Integrity instrumentation module.

Features of PIB-OSS:

* Hooks Google Play Integrity service calls in com.android.vending
* Per-app policy controls for request/response logging
* Error-only logging mode for cleaner debugging sessions
* Optional response rewrite mode for controlled failure testing
* Runtime log buffer with clear/export support
* Nix-friendly reproducible build workflow

For more information, check this wiki page: https://github.com/frknkrc44/PIB-OSS/wiki
